## 3. Building the data pipeline

**Design and engine roles.** The pipeline turns the raw corpus into one analysis table, `features.weekly`, with one row per stock and trading week. Each engine does the work it suits. Hadoop Streaming parses the raw sources and writes audit summaries: one job checks every price series, and one job reads each GDELT ZIP in a single mapper without expanding it. PySpark does the row-level work: cleaning, split screening, eligibility, labels and features. Hive registers every stage as a table and does two set-based transformations in SQL, the trading calendar and the weekly news measures. Spark reads all Hive tables through the shared metastore. Every run writes to new folders named by its run ID, and all table definitions are versioned scripts, so a fresh cluster can rebuild everything (Appendix A).

**Cleaning and rejected data.** Every raw price row goes to exactly one of two tables, clean or rejected, with the first failed rule as its reason. The rules cover missing or non-numeric values, non-positive prices, a high below the low, an open or close outside the day's range, duplicate stock-days, and dates outside the study window. Nothing is repaired or filled. Of 619,040 raw rows, 605,887 are clean, matching the brief's accepted count, and 13,153 are rejected: 13,130 fall after 29 December 2017 and 23 are invalid. The Streaming audit independently finds the same 11 missing and 12 inconsistent rows, which cross-checks both engines.

**Calendar, universe and splits.** A trading day is a date on which at least half of the series trade. A week starts on Friday, or the next trading day after a holiday. Features use closes up to the previous trading day (the cutoff), and the outcome runs from the start-day open to the next start-day open. The nine holiday weeks the calendar finds match the expected holiday Fridays. Weeks are assigned chronologically: 2013 to 2015 train (148 weeks), 2016 validation (52) and 2017 test (51). Four weeks whose outcome crosses a year end are excluded, so no target extends into a later period. A stock-week is eligible only if the stock has 61 consecutive clean closes ending at the cutoff and valid opens on both start dates. Gaps are kept as gaps, so a stock with a missing day stays ineligible until its window is clean again, and no value is ever filled from later data.

**Splits and corporate actions.** Prices are mostly split-adjusted, but not always. A screen flags overnight moves larger than 22% that sit near a split ratio, and records the volume change for each. Five moves were reviewed by hand. Discovery's two share classes, which halve on 7 August 2014, were confirmed and their affected stock-weeks excluded (28). Three news-driven moves were kept as genuine price changes.

**Labels and features.** The label is 1 when a stock's next-week source price ratio is above the median of that week's eligible stocks, which gives a 50% share in every split. The ratio and label are never features. Nine indicators cover reversal, momentum, volatility, relative volume and liquidity, using fixed windows. Each is also expressed as its percentile rank within the week, which uses same-week data only and fits no parameters, so it cannot leak information between splits. Learned preprocessing, such as scaling, is left to the model pipeline and fitted on training data only. Features were checked against an independent recalculation from the raw file for sample stock-weeks.

**Reconciliation.** Eligible stock-weeks are 65,086 (train), 25,720 (validation) and 25,531 (test), with 471 to 504 eligible stocks per week. Exclusions by reason are reported per split (Appendix B). Most come from short histories and late-listed stocks; only 11 come from missing opens. All 731 GDELT archives were read in one mapper each (52 minutes of the 59-minute run): 144,207,180 lines, all kept as events, with no corrupt archive, malformed line or duplicate, and all checksums matched. Pre-start news measures exist for 97 weeks: 17 late-2015 training weeks (after a 60-day baseline), all 52 validation weeks and 26 test weeks to June 2017.
